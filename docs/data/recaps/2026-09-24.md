# LASwank Rebounds From Quads to Win the Season's Biggest Field

*September 24, 2026 · NL Hold'em · $24+1 with a $5 bounty · 19 entries · $361 prize pool*

The largest field of Season 16 produced its biggest night for one player, and it began with a cooler. At 7:34 PM LASwank called jalt's all-in check-raise on a 6♥T♥6♠9♦ board with pocket kings. jalt held A♠6♣ for trip sixes, and the 6♦ river gave LASwank a full house but jalt four sixes. LASwank re-entered two minutes later and spent the next hour taking over Table 2. J♣T♠ turned a straight to bust Berkeley124's kings and nines. Q-J two pair finished Lagunator. Then Q♦J♥ filled up on a Q♠9♦Q♣J♠4♠ board, and LASwank's all-in reraise on the river got a call from ChoppedRiver's trip queens. LASwank brought 35,769 chips to the final table at 8:34 PM, more than three times the next-biggest stack.

The final table chipped away at that lead. ytrabbit's pocket tens made a set but lost to CrazyIdeas' bigger set of jacks. LawlessWC's pocket tens hit a set on the river against LASwank's Q-8 for a 25,818-chip pot, leaving LASwank with 11,485. LASwank won the next big hand when K-J held against LawlessWC's flush draw. They moved back to the top at 9:33 PM when K♣Q♣ made trip queens against Manzoniman's A♥K♥. Polarized616 had their own comeback, from 1,445 chips at 9:14 PM to the chip lead on the bubble. They won with pocket queens, K-9 and pocket jacks, and their pocket kings eliminated LawlessWC in sixth. With five left, all five stacks were between 13,000 and 22,000. Manzoniman went out on the bubble when their last 898 chips lost to LASwank and CrazyIdeas, who split the pot with matching A-6. LASwank's pocket sixes then beat winston's A-T, and winston finished fourth ($31.41).

Three-handed was close, with CrazyIdeas on 33,271, Polarized616 on 32,536 and LASwank on 29,193. It ended on one hand. LASwank moved all in with T♣3♣, CrazyIdeas called all in with 5♠2♦, and Polarized616 called with 4♦2♣. On the T♥9♥2♠ flop, LASwank's pair of tens was ahead of both opponents' pairs of deuces, and the 6♠ and 9♣ changed nothing. LASwank won the 93,232-chip main pot, the biggest pot of the season. CrazyIdeas finished third ($61.73), and Polarized616 was left with 1,768 chips. Polarized616 won two all-ins, the second with a turned club flush, before LASwank's A♦3♦ beat their 5♦2♦ at 10:00 PM. LASwank earned $162.45 and nine bounties worth $45, both season highs, and climbs from 13th to ninth. Polarized616's runner-up finish ($105.41) extends their season lead to 7.29 points over Scamp.
