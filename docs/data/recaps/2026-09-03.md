# LAWolfie's Pocket Tens Hold for Flatline Faithful #441

*September 3, 2026 · NL Hold'em · $24+1 with a $5 bounty · 11 entries · $209 prize pool*

The final table formed early, at 7:45 PM, when SvenGolly's T-7 two pair lost to LawlessWC's Q-T on 7♠T♥Q♠. LAWolfie set the tone a minute later. They moved all in on the river and then showed 6♠4♠, a missed straight draw. Fifteen minutes after that, LawlessWC flopped a straight with T-7 on 6♦8♠9♠ and stacked Scamp's 9-8 two pair for 10,408 chips. That left Scamp with 1,317. On the next hand Scamp moved all in with A-Q, the Q♦9♠Q♣ flop gave them trips, and they more than tripled up against two callers. At 8:27 PM Scamp did it again. Their all-in A-6 was called by LAWolfie's pocket kings and Berkeley124's pocket queens, and the turn ace won Scamp the main pot. LAWolfie's kings took the side pot and Berkeley124's bounty.

LAWolfie built a big lead from there. At 9:02 PM pocket sixes held against Manzoniman's A-8 on T♥T♦7♦9♦Q♠, eliminating them in sixth, and LAWolfie reached the bubble with 25,532 chips. Scamp had 15,653, LawlessWC 8,633 and Polarized616 5,182. LawlessWC got back in it by calling LAWolfie's all-in reraise with A♠5♠. The A♣ flop beat LAWolfie's pocket sevens and won LawlessWC a 13,566-chip pot. Polarized616 won two uncalled shoves before their Q♦J♦ ran into LawlessWC's A♣6♣ on an A-Q flop. Polarized616 finished fourth, the bubble, but kept the season lead. Three-handed, Scamp moved all in with J♠9♠, and LAWolfie called with T♠T♣. The 9♣2♣Q♠ flop gave Scamp a pair of nines, and the 7♣ and 6♣ gave LAWolfie a flush. Scamp went out in third ($41.80).

Heads-up started at 9:25 PM with LAWolfie ahead 32,220 to 22,780. LawlessWC won one pot with a reraise all in, but four minutes later LAWolfie bet a 9♠8♣4♦ flop with pocket tens. LawlessWC check-raised all in with A♥4♣ for bottom pair and an ace. LAWolfie called, the 2♥ and 5♥ changed nothing, and the 50,160-chip pot ended the night at 9:29 PM. LAWolfie earned $104.50 and $25 in bounties. The 8.44 points move them from 10th to second with 19.44, behind Polarized616 at 23.28. LawlessWC was runner-up for the second straight week ($62.70 plus $15 in bounties) and climbs to fifth.
