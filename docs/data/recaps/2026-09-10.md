# Manzoniman Wins a Three-Hand Heads-Up Duel for Flatline Faithful #442

*September 10, 2026 · NL Hold'em · $24+1 with a $5 bounty · 13 entries · $247 prize pool*

LASwank had the busiest night of anyone. Their Q-T made a straight on 8♠9♥J♠Q♥, but Manzoniman's K♠T♠ made a higher one, and LASwank's first entry was gone by 7:54 PM. On the second entry they did real damage. At 8:18 PM ChoppedRiver moved all in with A♦8♦, and CrazyIdeas and LASwank called. CrazyIdeas held pocket queens and bet the river, but LASwank's K♥Q♦ caught the T♣ for Broadway and eliminated both players. Twenty-four minutes later LASwank's K♠9♠ turned a straight to bust SvenGolly in sixth. Meanwhile LAWolfie knocked out jalt twice, first with pocket sixes and then with a turned Q♣J♣ straight against A-K. Season leader Polarized616 left in seventh when their K♦J♠ made jacks full on J♣2♦2♣A♥2♥ and lost to LAWolfie's A♣K♣, which made aces full.

The bubble was tight: Manzoniman had 19,669, LAWolfie 18,548, Scamp 18,135 and LASwank 8,648. LASwank moved all in over LAWolfie's four-bet with pocket jacks, LAWolfie called with A-K, and the A♣ flop ended LASwank's run in fourth. Three-handed, LAWolfie led with 33,762, but the other two traded big pots. Manzoniman moved all in on a 5♥7♠8♠ flop with 8-3, and top pair held against Scamp's pocket fours for 25,913 chips. Scamp doubled back with A-T against Q-J. At 9:16 PM Scamp's luck ran out: they called LAWolfie's 18,200 reraise with K♥J♠ against A♥J♣, the A♣ river paired LAWolfie, and Scamp finished third ($49.40).

Heads-up began with LAWolfie ahead 43,079 to 21,921, and the next three hands were all-ins. Manzoniman moved all in on the 8♠ turn with K♣3♥, LAWolfie called with T♥7♣ and an open-ended straight draw, and the 8♣ river paired the board, so Manzoniman's king kicker won a 43,842-chip pot. On the next hand Manzoniman moved all in with T♣4♦, and LAWolfie's Q♠9♣ paired on the flop to win 42,316 back. On the third, LAWolfie moved all in with 8♥2♣, and Manzoniman called with A♠J♣. The Q♦J♥A♣ flop gave Manzoniman two pair, and the A♥ turn and J♠ river made a full house. The tournament ended at 9:20 PM. Manzoniman earned $123.50 and $20 in bounties for their first win of Season 16 and climbs from 12th to seventh. LAWolfie collected $74.10 and five bounties and closed to 0.93 points behind Polarized616.
