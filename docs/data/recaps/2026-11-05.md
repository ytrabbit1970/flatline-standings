# LAWolfie Runs Over the Final Table to Win Flatline Faithful #450

The 5♣ turn added a flush draw, but the 7♠ river missed both. At 8:47 jalt moved in on the 4♥ turn with A♠8♥. LAWolfie called with A♥5♣, and the 5♦ river gave them aces and fives and a 13,497-chip pot.

That left four players, with three paid, and LAWolfie held 28,669 of the 60,000 chips in play. A8_Fold_Path burst the bubble at 8:58 when their 9♣8♣ all-in ran into CrazyIdeas' A♦4♦. The K♥T♦J♣ flop gave A8_Fold_Path an open-ended straight draw, but the A♣ turn and K♠ river missed it. Moments later CrazyIdeas' pocket fives made trips on the turn against LASwank's K-Q, and LASwank took third ($43.20). Heads-up began with LAWolfie ahead 42,635 to 17,365. On J♦J♠6♠T♥3♥ both players had a pair of jacks with an ace, and LAWolfie's seven outkicked the board's six. LAWolfie collected $108 and $25 in bounties for their second win of Season 16. The 8.48 points move them from ninth to fourth.
