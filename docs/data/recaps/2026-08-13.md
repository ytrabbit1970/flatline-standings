# A8_Fold_Path Collects Seven Bounties and Flatline Faithful #438

*August 13, 2026 · NL Hold'em · $24+1 with a $5 bounty · 13 entries · $247 prize pool*

A8_Fold_Path set the tone four minutes in. Holding pocket fives on a 5♥A♠Q♥9♠4♦ board, they let CrazyIdeas lead the river with aces up, then moved all in. That was the first of seven bounties they would collect on the night. That final table produced a run of identical hands. SvenGolly and ChoppedRiver split a pot holding K-Q against K-Q, then split again 14 minutes later when both had pocket kings on a board that made queens full of kings.

Eight minutes later ytrabbit moved all in on the A♦ turn with K-9, and ChoppedRiver called with A-T for trip aces. The K♠ river gave ytrabbit only two pair, and they finished fourth, one place out of the money. In between, A8_Fold_Path and ChoppedRiver split a 41,328-chip pot when both turned the same king-high straight with Q-J.

Three-handed, ChoppedRiver led with 34,334. A8_Fold_Path called SvenGolly's all-in flop bet with pocket sixes on 5♠2♠5♣, and the 5♥ turn gave them a full house over SvenGolly's trip fives with A-T. SvenGolly finished third ($49.40), and A8_Fold_Path took a 38,008-chip pot. Heads-up was over in three all-ins. ChoppedRiver doubled with 6♥5♥ to lead 48,568 to 16,432. A8_Fold_Path's Q♥4♥ caught a queen on the river to pull back almost even. On the next hand ChoppedRiver moved all in with T-8, and A8_Fold_Path's pocket sevens held on 5♠3♥5♣3♦Q♣. The tournament ended at 9:42 PM. A8_Fold_Path earned $123.50 and $35 in bounties and moves into fourth with 8.87 points. ChoppedRiver ($74.10 plus $15) climbs from 13th to third. ytrabbit keeps the lead with 14.22 points after two weeks.
