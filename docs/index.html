How Points Work

Each week's points depend on your finish, the field size and the prize pool:

pts = √(entries ÷ place) × (1 + log(pool ÷ entries + ¼))² ÷ (1 + log(entries + ¼))

Re-entries count once: your final finish is what scores. Bigger fields are worth more, and winning beats everything.
